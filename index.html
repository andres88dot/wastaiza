Monte Carlo Simulation: Approximating π

This interactive experiment estimates π using random points uniformly distributed in the square \([-1,1]\times[-1,1]\). The proportion of points inside the unit circle approximates the ratio of their areas: \[ \pi \approx 4\,\frac{N_{\mathrm{inside}}}{N_{\mathrm{total}}}. \] Choose the number of points and run the simulation to observe how the approximation evolves.
